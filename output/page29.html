7-Eleven made a micro-K-drama to promote its new noms

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

7-Eleven is going full microdrama to promote its latest munchies lineup.

Starting Nov. 2, 60-second episodes of A Convenient Love will pop up on 7-Eleven’s TikTok and Instagram channels to promote its new “Flavors of Korea” menu.

The mini K-drama follows two classmates whose destinies intertwine during late-night runs to (of course) the local 7-Eleven. A short trailer captures a 7-Eleven employee/classic Korean uncle telling the two not-so-star-crossed lovers that they should notice: opposites attract.

He delivers this line holding what is arguably the centerpiece of 7-Eleven’s new collection: a KBBQ pizza that’s an official partnership with Korean frozen food brand bibigo.

“K-dramas run on heightened moments. And some of the most memorable ones happen over something as simple as a late-night meal at a convenience store,” Danny Lee, A Convenient Love‘s director, said in a statement. “That’s what made 7-Eleven’s new Korean-inspired menu a natural fit. We built 7 vertical episodes around it and shot all of them in a real 7-Eleven with the food at every turning point. In this genre the style is core to the storytelling, so we leaned into those familiar devices with some American cinematic flair, from the characters and humor to the visual details and, of course, the food from 7-Eleven.”

Pizza aside, the Flavors of Korea collection includes a gochujang remix on 7-Eleven’s iconic egg salad sando, gochujang boneless wings, bibigo spicy chicken dumplings, a KBBQ taquito, a KBBQ hot dog, and somyeon with kimchi.

7-Eleven will drop Convenient Love episodes throughout November, and each installment will “feature a limited-time discount on a spotlighted menu item,” according to a press release.

We have of course seen other brands capitalizing on the microseries trend, but 7-Eleven might be hitting the sweet spot. Microdramas got off the ground in Asia, and K-dramas specifically (long- or short-form) are an international sensation. Combining the two could push A Convenient Love to a level other branded series have yet to reach. We’re curious to see how it pays off.

Yo, 7-Eleven: Hit us up with performance data when the series wraps. (We also wouldn’t mind a sando or two.)

NASA reaches out to commercial space industry for help saving legendary Hubble Space Telescope

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Science  |  Read Source →

Sad news for space aficionados: the famous Hubble Space Telescope, responsible for some of the most jaw-dropping images of our solar system taken more than three decades since its launch into low-Earth orbit in 1990, is on a slow path towards a fiery fate. At its present rate of altitude loss, NASA's most famous telescope is expected to re-enter Earth's atmosphere sometime in 2033 or 2034, at which point it will come crashing down like a comet, according to Space.com, possibly endangering human life on its descent

It doesn't have to be that way, though. If NASA had more room in its budget, it could repeat its 2002 Space Shuttle Columbia mission STS-109 and give the aging telescope a nudge into a higher orbit, extending its lifespan. In desperation, NASA has put out a Request for Information (RFI) to the fast-growing commercial space sector, hoping for aid to either boost Hubble's altitude and extend its lifespan or else guide its descent to a safe crash site, such as the middle of the ocean.

According to the RFI, a successful orbital boost would require an ascent of more than 370 miles (600 kilometers), which is a significant increase in elevation and an astonishingly difficult challenge from a purely technical perspective. 

In a statement on the NASA website, Nicky Fox, an associate administrator of the Science Mission Directorate, spoke to the growing power and influence of the private space sector: "Commercial industry has rapidly evolved in recent years to offer new capabilities for in-space satellite capture and servicing. Given the time we have until Hubble’s anticipated re-entry, we are eager to explore the breadth of possibilities to boost Hubble to a higher orbit while we develop the flagship mission to follow."  

NASA administrator Jared Isaacman spoke of a duty to both "the American taxpayer" and "the scientific community" to explore all possible ways to extend Hubble's life, and the project is likely to attract serious interest from companies like Elon Musk's SpaceX and Peter Beck's Rocket Lab, which became famous for successfully catching a used rocket with a helicopter. 

It wouldn't even be the first time that the commercial space sector has lent NASA a helping hand. In March of last year, stranded astronauts Suni Williams and Butch Wilmore were rescued from the International Space Station with assistance from SpaceX's Dragon spacecraft.

Let's hope history repeats itself and NASA finds a willing collaborator, because Hubble's insights over more than a three decades have radically altered our understanding of the universe we inhabit and inspired new generations to look to the stars.

A serious security flaw could let hackers disrupt servers that help run Cardano, raising concerns for holders staking roughly 21.5 billion ADA, about 58% of the circulating supply.

So far, no Cardano pool attack has been reported.

What Is the New Cardano Security Risk?

On October 30, Cardano coordinating group Intersect warned pool operators about CVE-2026-98432, a Linux vulnerability rated 9.8 out of 10 for severity.

Linux runs many of the servers that produce Cardano blocks. The bug lets specially crafted internet requests confuse how a server handles memory. It could crash the machine or, in certain configurations, let an outsider take control without a password.

On October 27, Anthropic said its Claude AI had developed such an attack against servers using multipath TCP, a networking feature. Its test took 53 minutes. Public test code later appeared, although it did not demonstrate a complete takeover.

Can Hackers Steal ADA or Stop Staking Rewards?

Cardano staking allows holders to back a pool that produces blocks and earns rewards. The ADA never leaves the holder’s wallet.

“Your ada stays in your wallet and remains spendable at any time,” Cardano states.

Hijacking a pool therefore does not give hackers ownership of its delegators’ coins. But taking its server offline could reduce the blocks produced and the rewards paid out.

“Poor stake pool performance decreases the number of rewards that a pool and its members receive,” Cardano explains.

No stolen funds or reward losses have been linked to this flaw. ADA’s price could suffer if disruptions became widespread, but there is no established direct market impact.

Are Cardano Operators Protected?

Patches are arriving unevenly. Intersect has urged operators to check their servers, then update and reboot.

Morgan Wallen, Garth Brooks, Taylor Swift & More Artists With Most Weeks at No. 1 on Top Country Albums Chart: Full List

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Morgan Wallen’s I’m the Problem leads Billboard‘s Top Country Albums chart (dated Nov. 7) for the 51st nonconsecutive week. This ups Wallen’s career tally of weeks at No. 1 to 237, allowing him to extend his lead as the artist with the most weeks at No. 1 in the chart’s 62-year history.

Wallen’s achievement is especially impressive because he has amassed this hefty total of weeks at No. 1 in such a short amount of time. He first topped the chart a little more than six years ago — on Aug. 15, 2020.

Top Country Albums originated as Hot Country Albums in the Jan. 11, 1964 issue of Billboard. Johnny Cash’s Ring of Fire (The Best of Johnny Cash) headed the inaugural chart. The compilation was well-timed: “Ring of Fire” had headed Hot Country Songs for seven weeks the previous summer and had crossed over to reach the top 20 on the Billboard Hot 100.

The chart was named Hot Country LP’s from 1968 to 1984, when vinyl LPs were king.

Twenty-one artists have logged 50 or more weeks at No. 1 on Top Country Albums. Male solo artists dominate, as you might expect, though three female solo artists have achieved the feat, as have two groups. (One of those groups is an all-female group.) Only one Black artist has amassed 50 or more weeks at No. 1, though that may change as country becomes more inclusive.
