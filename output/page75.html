Headquarters: Remote - GA

Who we are

Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations™ Cloud, which is a platform that enables organizations that depend on physical operations to harness Internet of Things (IoT) data to develop actionable insights and improve their operations. At Samsara, we are helping improve the safety, efficiency and sustainability of the physical operations that power our global economy. Representing more than 40% of global GDP, these industries are the infrastructure of our planet, including agriculture, construction, field services, transportation, and manufacturing — and we are excited to help digitally transform their operations at scale.

Working at Samsara means you’ll help define the future of physical operations and be on a team that’s shaping an exciting array of product solutions, including Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring.   • Ability and willingness to make 50 cold calls a day.
  • At least 6 months of sales experience and/or sales prospecting ability. This can be sales related coursework, sales competitions, etc.

Wrexham are stalling under Phil Parkinson – and evidence suggest change is needed: Opinion

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Wrexham boss Phil Parkinson is starting to see pressure mount on him with the club 18th in the Championship after 13 games.

Wrexham have had a meteoric rise up English football in recent years, and a lot of that has been down to Phil Parkinson. While many point to the money spent by the club as the main reason, the job Parkinson has done to keep the them flying up the divisions has been excellent.

Since arriving in July 2021, he has guided the Welsh side to three consecutive promotions, taking them from the National League to the Championship in just three seasons. He also secured a club-record seventh-place finish in their first campaign back in the second tier, narrowly missing out on the play-offs last season.

Photo by Timmy96, via Wikimedia Commons, licensed under CC0 1.0.

However, the pressure is beginning to build on the 58-year-old. Wrexham have struggled to replicate their previous success in the Championship this season, sitting around 18th after 13 games and suffering a disappointing run of results.

Parkinson has called for honest self-assessment, but questions are increasingly being asked about whether he is the right man to take Wrexham to the next level.

Wrexham’s recruitment under Parkinson has seen them stall

Wrexham spent approximately £33million on 13 players ahead of their first Championship campaign, before committing at least another £23.5million on seven senior additions last summer. While Josh Windass, Dom Hyam and Kieffer Moore have delivered, other expensive arrivals have struggled to make an impact.

With Parkinson holding significant influence over recruitment, responsibility for the squad’s shortcomings inevitably falls partly on his shoulders. Injuries to Windass and Nathan Broadhead have exposed a lack of attacking depth, while questions remain over whether the latest additions represent genuine upgrades.

The appointment of Steve Nickson as head of recruitment suggests Wrexham recognise the need to improve their scouting structure. Their ambitions are increasingly ambitious, and a more sophisticated recruitment operation could help them compete with established Championship clubs.

There is also the question of Parkinson’s tactical approach. His direct, physical style helped Wrexham dominate the lower divisions, but there are growing doubts over whether it offers enough variety against Championship opposition. The early signs suggest that his style has caught up on them, which is something cannot really afford.

The Verdict: Wrexham just need to look at Coventry City as a guide

Coventry City provide an interesting example of why a change might be necessary. Mark Robins delivered an extraordinary rise, taking the Sky Blues from League Two to the Championship and establishing them as promotion contenders.

However, after a difficult start to the 2024/25 campaign, he was dismissed. Frank Lampard subsequently took charge and guided Coventry into the Premier League in 2025/26. That is not to suggest changing managers guarantees success, but it demonstrates how a fresh voice can unlock another level.

Parkinson deserves enormous credit for everything he has achieved, and Wrexham’s owners Ryan Reynolds and Rob McElhenney have previously made clear their faith in him. However, loyalty cannot become an obstacle to progress.

Wrexham have invested heavily and have Premier League ambitions. If Parkinson cannot translate that spending into consistent Championship performances, the owners may should look to replace him with someone who can.

Paper Rex has parted with captain d4v41 and Jinggg, two Masters Toronto winners, after an early exit at Champions 2026. With its VCT Pacific partner place secure, the Singapore organisation is rebuilding by choice as free agency opens to every region.

Paper Rex has broken up the core of its VALORANT roster. The Singapore organisation is parting with its captain and one of its best-known players as the 2027 season approaches. d4v41 had been with Paper Rex since its founding in 2020. He played first on its CS:GO roster, then moved with the team to VALORANT. Jinggg joined on 28 September 2021.

Neither player has named a destination. In the farewell video Paper Rex released, d4v41 said he was moving to a new team. Jinggg wrote on X that he was excited about what comes next. d4v41 also asked fans to keep supporting the organisation after both have gone.

A strong 2026 ended with an early Champions exit

The split follows a season that faded at the end. Paper Rex were runners-up at both Masters Santiago and Masters London earlier in 2026. At Champions in Shanghai, they lost 2-0 to LOUD in the upper-bracket quarter-finals on 7 October. A 2-1 defeat to T1 in lower-bracket round one ended their run on 9 October. The event carried a $2,250,000 prize pool. LOUD went on to beat Team Vitality in the final.

The pair leave with a long record together. Jinggg helped Paper Rex to second place at Masters Copenhagen 2022. He was also part of the run to the Champions 2023 final, where the team were runners-up. Liquipedia lists both players in the line-up that won VCT 2025 Masters Toronto.

A secure partner place makes this a rebuild by choice

The changes are not forced by the league. The organisation is owned by IMPLS Entertainment Pte Ltd. Riot has cut the number of partner teams in each region from ten to eight for 2027–28. That leaves 32 across the four regions.

The Pacific format rewards that security. The eight partners receive seeded places at Kickoff in January 2027. Four further places go to teams that come through open qualifiers. Paper Rex can therefore reshape its roster without needing to qualify.

Two Masters winners on the open market could matter well beyond the Pacific. EMEA’s eight partners for 2027 are Fnatic, BBL Esports, FUT Esports, Team Heretics, Team Liquid, Gentle Mates, Karmine Corp and Team Vitality. Under Riot’s open qualifier rules, most regions require three of a team’s five players to come from that region. Teams must also keep three of five players between events to carry their progress forward. Those limits will frame how far any club can rebuild around new signings.

Luciano Spalletti struck a defiant tone on the eve of Juventus-Napoli, a game seen as a must-win. He also backed the club's response to the defeat at Genoa, including a squad retreat.

Spalletti made his stance clear on the eve of Juventus-Napoli. Anyone expecting him to retreat even slightly, he warned, should expect something different.

Sunday’s game is at the Allianz Stadium. Spalletti said the side want to win back the respect that has been taken from them. Several outlets say his job is on the line.

Carnevali’s address won the coach’s full approval

The build-up has been shaped by the club’s response to the defeat at Genoa. Ehizibue scored in the 12th minute and Baldanzi in the 28th. After a long meeting between chief executive Giovanni Carnevali and the squad, Spalletti announced a retreat. The team would stay at the Continassa from Friday evening until the Napoli game.

Spalletti called Carnevali’s address perfect and said he had hit the right notes. He has never been keen on training-camp retreats. In these circumstances, though, he said one could help the group become more compact again.

The coach also played down any need for public backing. He needs no reassurance from the club, only a reaction from the team, Eurosport reported. He added that he is not asking his players for anything beyond their capabilities.

Fourteen points leaves Juventus short of last season’s pace

After nine rounds, this season’s tally trails the 15 Juventus had at the same stage last season and the 17 under Motta in 2024/25. It is above the 13 under Allegri in 2022/23, according to Tuttosport.

Recent form has been uneven. The last five league results were a 2-0 home win over Atalanta, a 1-1 draw at Cagliari and a 3-0 home defeat by Lazio. A 2-0 win at Lecce and the loss at Genoa complete the run. Two of those five games ended in defeat, and Juventus failed to score in both.

Napoli arrive without their coach. The Serie A sporting judge banned Massimiliano Allegri for two matches over disrespectful remarks to the officials in the 3-3 draw with Roma. The ban covers the Monza and Juventus games.

Atlético Madrid won 2-0 at Levante on Saturday, making it a milestone afternoon for Diego Simeone. Barcelona are next up in the league, and one more victory would give him the coaching record on his own.

Atlético Madrid’s trip to Valencia ended with a milestone for their coach. It took the Argentine 565 LaLiga matches in charge to reach the mark, according to Club Atlético de Madrid.

The visitors went in at the interval a goal up. Álex Baena supplied the pass for their second. Earlier, Morten Hjulmand had a goal ruled out by VAR in the 17th minute. Levante coach Luís Castro was shown a yellow card for dissent a minute later.

Levante made the chances but Oblak kept them out

Atlético had more of the ball, with 54% possession and 560 passes to Levante’s 450. Simeone made two double changes. Jonathan David and Sørloth replaced Lee Kang-In and Julián Álvarez in the 67th minute. Le Normand and Lookman came on for Barrios and Baena in the 76th.
