By: Isak Möller

As is often the case with the derby win, there were a lot of fun facts and stats to delve into after the full-time whistle, including a free-kick rarity from Diego Moreira. 

Many headed into the Derby della Madonnina with a lot of worry considering Inter’s strong start to the season, as well as Milan’s mid-week defeat to Bologna. However, it turned out to be a great evening for the Rossoneri, who clinched all three points in a 3-1 win.

Diego Moreira played a key role in the success, bagging one goal and two assists, while Ruben Amorim got his tactics right as well. In short, it was just what they needed and as is often the case after a huge win, a lot of fun stats emerged afterwards.

Misleading key stats

Starting with the general stats, it’s clear that Milan made the most of their chances with a 0.66 xG and three goals scored. Moreira’s free-kick obviously didn’t yield a high xG, nor did Adrien Rabiot’s tidy effort, and Christian Pulisic’s header wasn’t super easy to pull off.

Inter had some good chances from close range, parried by Mike Maignan, so that is probably why their xG is higher. Alessandro Bastoni’s header was also an easy one, unlike Pulisic’s, but it was nice to see Milan be the efficient team for once.

The possession is linked to the above, really, as Milan sat back a lot after scoring their goals. Tactically, they played it to perfection and Inter didn’t really create much with all that possession in the end.

Unbeaten run extended

This deserves a section of its own because Milan’s recent record in the derby is truly amazing. After losing six games on the trot, the Rossoneri have certainly turned things around with six wins in the last eight encounters and no defeats!

Now the Inter fans can feel some of the pain that we experienced a while back… It’s not just that the Nerazzurri as a club have been really poor against Milan, it’s also that Cristian Chivu has failed completely.

Winning trophies is the most important thing, he’s absolutely right, but there is no question that the derby record is bothering him. As reported by Opta, he’s only the third manager to lose his first three derbies after Leonardo (2009-2011) and Massimo Giacomini (1979-1980).

Free-kicks, fast goals and a midfield king

Like we said at the start, a lot of fun facts emerged from the win last night and giving each of them a section would be a little silly. From a rare free-kick goal in the derby to a midfielder in red-hot form, we have listed them below.

Moreira’s free-kick: Since Opta started collecting the stats in 2004-05, Milan have only scored three direct free-kick goals against Inter in all competitions, with Moreira’s being the latest one. Pirlo (2007) and Theo Hernandez (2025) were the first two.

Photo by Marco Luzzani/Getty Images

Pulisic’s speedy start: Pulisic’s goal is the fastest Milan have scored in a league derby against Inter since the 2010-11 season, when Alexandre Pato found the back of the net after a mere 43 seconds. Talk about getting off the right foot.

Rabiot’s goal contributions: Rabiot has become the third Milan midfielder to have at least one goal contribution in five consecutive league games since 2004-05, following in the footsteps of Hakan Calhanoglu (in 2020) and Ricky Kaka (2004 and 2008).

Tron CEO Says Ethereum’s “Fragile Double Top” Prompted Him to Sell 80-90% of ETH Holdings

In the Wu Blockchain interview, Justin Sun shared his bold decision to sell 80%-90% of his Ethereum holdings and allocate his portfolio to a selection of US crypto stocks. This comes as the TRON CEO sees massive opportunity in the US market amid what he termed a sluggish performance of Ethereum cryptocurrency.

Why Justin Sun sold his Ether holdings

The founder and CEO of TRON Foundation recently announced that he sold most of his Ethereum holdings. In the recent interview, Justin Sun confirmed that he sold 80%-90% of his personal Ether assets. The interesting event marks his allocation strategy to maximize returns in the US equity market conditions, as he expects US crypto stocks to outperform Ethereum in the near future.

Sun’s decision to withdraw from the market is seen as a personal choice, triggered by what he described as Ethereum’s ‘double top pattern’.  According to the TRON executive, Ether appears to have formed a fragile double top pattern. His clever analysis identified a continuous, bearish pattern that has built a persistent downtrend, which has been hampering profit-making in the ETH market.

The persistence of the ‘fragile double top pattern’ prompted him to cut his Ether positions sharply, while leaving his Bitcoin holdings unchanged, the executive revealed.

Sun on US crypto stocks investments

Sun’s decision to sell huge chunks of his Ethereum holdings and allocate the funds to US crypto stocks is driven by undervaluation and diversification factors.

He believes that Ethereum is currently non-performing, presenting him with an opportunity to invest the funds in other assets with growth potential. By allocating his Ether funds to US crypto stocks, the renowned investor aims to diversify his portfolio and mitigate risks associated with investing in non-performing assets.

Sun believes that U.S.-listed crypto-related stocks are undervalued, yet have strong market capability. In another Wu Blockchain interview shared earlier today, the TRON CEO revealed his decision to invest in the US-based crypto-related stock, World Liberty Financial (WLFI). In the podcast, he reaffirmed that if given another chance, he would inevitably reinvest in WLFI.

In November 2024, Sun bought $30 million in WLFI tokens, an investment that made him the largest investor in the DeFi platform backed by US President Trump.

Solana Walks a Tightrope as ETF Outflows Persist and Spot Volume Turns “Heating”

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Solana is under fresh pressure after falling below the important $107 support level. Analysts have pointed to heavy ETF outflows and plummeting spot trading volume as the latest developments pushing the market back into a defensive phase.

Warning signs for Solana  

SOL is currently caught between a rock and a hard place, hovering around $95 on Monday, Oct. 19, 2026. The price has fallen 5.81% over the past week as short-term weakness battles a longer-term recovery. The asset is facing pressure to move above the crucial $107 support zone. Analysts believe this could decide whether the market falls further or renew its recovery.

The asset is facing massive liquidity pressure as persistent outflows from spot ETFs create sustained downward pressure on the fifth-largest cryptocurrency. Recent analysis from analyst Sovereign Yield shows Solana is trapped below its critical support region of $107, suggesting potential volatility ahead for SOL investors.

Persistent ETF outflows signal that institutional demand for SOL has weakened significantly in October, according to data from Sovereign Yield. U.S. spot Solana ETFs registered $25.52 million in net outflows in the week ending Oct. 16. This follows another $ 24.81 million in outflows the previous week. This marks two straight weeks of unprecedented withdrawal levels and net outflows. According to Sovereign Yield’s report, this shift has significantly weakened institutional buying appetite that previously supported price rises.

Meanwhile, a report from CryptoQuant analysts indicates further difficulties in the Solana market. According to the CryptoQuant data, spot Solana trading volume shows a bearish shift, as spot selling volume has surpassed spot buying volume for the first time in more than six months. This trend reversal signals a shift in market sentiment.

According to the data, Solana’s spot volume bubble map signals weakening buying interest, rising investor caution, and more common risk-off positioning in the market.

Analysts hint at what comes next  

The two key metrics above (from Sovereign Yield and CryptoQuant analysts) show that demand for Solana ETFs and spot selling has weakened in recent weeks. This indicates lower investor confidence.

Bearish signals have risen sharply, rising concerns about a greater correction. If selling pressure continues, the analysts believe Solana could drop below the $100 level soon.

Hyundai Is Spending $500 Million to Build Its Next Tucson in Alabama

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Reinvesting For The Future

American car production has always been an important part of competing in the local automotive market. Any car maker that wants to be taken seriously and sell in decent numbers needs a manufacturing plant, which is why most major brands have multiple facilities scattered across the U.S.

The Trump administration has been pushing its agenda to ensure that American jobs are safe and increase. As part of the government’s plan, it aims to ensure America keeps building cars, and we’re seeing carmakers respond with new or refreshed plants to protect the manufacturing future.

Hyundai

Hyundai’s $500 Million Alabama Expansion

Hyundai’s U.S. base is in Montgomery, Alabama, with a 2 million square foot footprint and the capacity to produce 400,000 cars and 700,000 engines annually. Yet the South Korean manufacturer just announced it wants to expand the Alabama plant with a sizable $500 million investment. The new investment will “support future vehicle production, expand engine manufacturing capabilities, and advance next-generation propulsion technologies at the Montgomery manufacturing facility.”

The fresh capital injection will bring Hyundai’s total investment in Alabama to a respectable $2.2 billion since it began operations in 2005. Hyundai isn’t done yet, with plans to bring its investment in the U.S. to $2.6 billion between 2025 and 2028, as Hyundai Motor Company President and CEO José Muñoz announced. Mark Rader, president and CEO of Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama, said the new investment will ensure HMMA remains a cornerstone of Hyundai’s North American manufacturing operations for years to come.

Hyundai

New Cars and New Engines

In essence, the new investment will retool the Alabama plant to support production of Extended Range Electric Vehicle (EREV) technology and the new Tucson and refreshed Santa Fe models. These two cars are important for the Hyundai brand in the U.S., with the Tucson remaining their best-selling model and the Santa Fe close behind. According to the Hyundai U.S. group, the all-new Tucson is set to begin production before the year closes out.

Hyundai’s EREV technology is set to usher in a new era for the brand. The EREV tech that Hyundai will develop and build promises to be different from the current crop of range-extending powertrains. Hyundai’s EREV aims to be more compact, lighter, and more energy-efficient.
