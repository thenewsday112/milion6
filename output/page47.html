Harry Kane&#8217;s former EFL manager details unusual penalty practice technique that saw him refuse help

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Harry Kane had a unique approach to penalty practice early in his career, a former manager of his has revealed.

The Ballon d’Or winner has built a reputation with his clinical spot-kicks for club and country.

Kane missed his second penalty of the season against Borussia DortmundGetty

However, Kane’s trademark precision deserted him during Bayern Munich’s 2-2 draw with Borussia Dortmund on Saturday night.

The England captain struck the crossbar from 12 yards, missing the chance to double his side’s lead.

Bayern would go on to be pegged back twice as they were held at the Allianz Arena.

It is already his second miss of the season having hit the woodwork for the Three Lions against Spain in September.

Kane’s penalty practice routine

Kane’s former Leyton Orient boss Russell Slade has now lifted the lid on the player’s penalty routine at the start of his senior career.

The 66-year-old signed a 17-year-old Kane on loan from Tottenham Hotspur back in January 2011.

He went on to score five goals in 18 appearances during his five-month stay at Brisbane Road, having been handed his professional debut by Slade.

None of those strikes were from 12 yards, though, despite Kane often practicing his spot-kicks after training, Slade has revealed.

Speaking to Shaun Custis and Henry Winter on Sunday Edition, he recalled: “He would spend 20 minutes, half an hour, just practicing the run up and exactly where he was placing the ball, time after time.

“I actually remember asking him one day, ‘do you need a keeper?’ He said, ‘no, thank you, it’s ok.’ It was just about his run up and where he was going to plant the ball.”

Slade has revealed Kane would practice penalties without a goalkeeperGetty

Slade was also asked if he could see Kane’s character straight away when he joined Orient.

He added: “Yes, absolutely. Very calm kid. Very dedicated boy. Would ask maybe the odd question.

“I think it was important that senior players, he quickly got a relationship with them and he wanted knowledge.

“He had a thirst for that knowledge and understanding of the game at a very early age.”

The 33-year-old had scored two penalties since the miss against Spain before his wayward effort on Saturday.

It marks the 16th time in his career he has failed to score a penalty during a competitive match.

Kane scored five goals in 18 appearances during a loan spell at Orient aged 17Getty

Kane has scored 113 penalties from his 129 attempts. The most recent of those converted efforts came against Arsenal in the Champions League last month.

2026 Tesla Model 3 Is Getting Cybercab Tech

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The Cybercab might be a robotaxi, but some of its hardware is coming to cars you can actually buy. In a bid to simplify building and powering its cars, Tesla is looking to incorporate certain components designed for its Cybercab in its passenger vehicles.

What Changes

Tesla

According to Drive Tesla Canada, Nick Verzini, Tesla’s head of drive unit engineering, said on a Munro Live factory tour that a new printed circuit board assembly and power stage will enter Model 3 production in 2026. They come from the inverter Tesla designed for the Cybercab it has started building.

The inverter turns the battery’s DC power into AC that spins the motor, so it affects efficiency and cost. Verzini said the new design has substantially fewer electronic components than the current Model 3 inverter, though no figure was given, and it accepts parts from multiple suppliers. When asked whether the whole unit could fit a Model Y drive unit, he said: “We kept the entire interface.” Tesla hasn’t announced a Model Y change as yet.

2026 Tesla Model Y Performance

Tesla

What It Means For Tesla Owners

Don’t expect a range jump. According to reports, efficiency, range, and price figures remain the same. What fewer parts and multiple suppliers could mean, though Tesla hasn’t promised it, is simpler and cheaper-to-build cars, with less exposure to supply chain volatility. For the Cybercab, Tesla’s 2025 Impact Report reportedly targets at least 6.1 miles per kWh, an efficiency figure that is crucial for a car meant to run all day.

What To Watch For

Tesla

Not everything carries over. The Cybercab’s front-mounted motor uses an 11:1 gear ratio, versus about 9:1 in many other Teslas, and swaps the electric oil pump for a mechanical one. Motor cooling channels also move into a cheaper aluminium housing. Those changes aren’t confirmed for other models. The real test is whether or not these changes will result in real-world gains in reliability, pricing, and/or efficiency.
