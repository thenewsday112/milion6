Spalletti: ‘Juventus players deserve credit for victory, but we need fans by our side’

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Luciano Spalletti said Juventus players ‘deserve all the credit’ for the 2-0 win over Napoli, but hits back at the boo boys. ‘We need our fans by our side, the real face of this stadium.’

The Bianconeri mood was dire going into this game, especially after the 2-0 defeat to relegation battlers Genoa on Wednesday evening.

Juan Cabal hit the woodwork, and Guglielmo Vicario needed a stunning early save on Lorenzo Lucca, but there were two moments of magic late on to seal the 2-0 victory.

Kerim Alajbegovic and Jeremie Boga hit screamers from outside the box, around a Luca Marianucci second bookable offence.

Spalletti stands up for Juventus players

TURIN, ITALY – SEPTEMBER 20: Luciano Spalletti, Manager of Juventus, looks on before the Serie A match between Juventus and Atalanta BC at Allianz Stadium on September 20, 2026 in Turin, Italy. (Photo by Valerio Pennicino/Getty Images)

At the final whistle, the coach pulled his staff members into a massive embrace.

“They suffer along with me, they too work from morning till night, I am the first to arrive and the last to leave, but they are usually there with me for most of it,” Spalletti told DAZN Italia.

“However, the credit this evening must go to the team. They managed to dig into themselves and find that desire to take the first step, to go forward, think positive, and then they managed it in the second half with a few adjustments.

“I think tonight they fully deserved the victory, restoring some of the self-esteem in the football they can play.”

TURIN, ITALY – NOVEMBER 01: Jeremie Boga of Juventus celebrates scoring his team’s second goal during the Serie A match between Juventus and SSC Napoli at Allianz Stadium on November 01, 2026 in Turin, Italy. (Photo by Valerio Pennicino/Getty Images)

It was for long spells far from a convincing performance, but this result can be a massive confidence-booster going forward, so how did it come about?

“The most important speech was made by Carnevali, because they are not accustomed to hearing his voice, so that alone forced the players to prick up their ears, listen and absorb those words, as it was the first time he’d done that,” revealed Spalletti.

“All we can do is be by their side, but there are players who genuinely plunge into this spiral and lose confidence in everything they do, even the most normal things. When there’s a misplaced simple pass or hesitation in making a decision, which is worse than making the wrong decision, that lack of confidence gets transferred to the rest of the team.

“Napoli had started the match well, we hit back two or three times, but as the game wore on, the lads did really well in making the right choices, pushing forward, thinking positive and leaving space behind them that is hard to defend against. The players deserve all the credit this evening.”

TURIN, ITALY – NOVEMBER 01: Kevin De Bruyne of SSC Napoli is challenged by Weston McKennie of Juventus during the Serie A match between Juventus and SSC Napoli at Allianz Stadium on November 01, 2026 in Turin, Italy. (Photo by Valerio Pennicino/Getty Images)

There were some fresh concerns, as Weston McKennie went off at half-time with an adductor issue, while Alajbegovic was spotted on the bench with an ice pack strapped to his calf after the goal.

When Spalletti was asked if this Juventus side can be structured to win with the current group or if they need big investments in future, he was not impressed.

“I know the game you are playing, with the pressure to put on the other, to have an advantage. At this moment, we are not structured in anything, because tonight I had to take McKennie off, Sarr had a fever and couldn’t play, all the substitutions I made were forced ones, and we’ve got to play again on Thursday.”

TURIN, ITALY – NOVEMBER 01: Players of Juventus applaud the fans after the team’s victory in the Serie A match between Juventus and SSC Napoli at Allianz Stadium on November 01, 2026 in Turin, Italy. (Photo by Valerio Pennicino/Getty Images)

Fans in the Allianz Stadium were shouting insults and calling for the team to show guts from the very first minute of tonight’s match with Napoli, with a protest outside the stadium before kick-off.

“We’ve just got to keep going. This is a moment we have to handle, then you can talk about what you want. Right now, we must be solid, along with a stadium whose real face is that of the second half, fighting alongside the team,” he added.

“You can say Spalletti’s no good, that player’s no good, get rid of everyone, but right now we need to be united, and this stadium must return to being our fortress. Even if we don’t make them that happy, we still need our fans by our side, so we thank all those today who applauded us and stayed with us.

“This is a moment where we need to hold hands and stand together, try to push forward and be positive, which isn’t easy when there are so few of us available. It was a victory deserved by the players, who deserve a big round of applause. Well done, lads.”

Could Tuesday’s Vote Reshape the Data Center Trade?

SystemD's Creator Opens Signups for Private Preview of Amutable's Cryptographically Verified Linux Systems

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →
SystemD creator Lennart Poettering left Red Hat for Microsoft in 2022, but 10 months ago left to co-found Amutable a company that aims to build cryptographically verified Linux systems that stay trusted over time. It's FOSS calls it "a read-only, image-based system where every machine produces signed reports of its own state" — and signups are now open for a private preview: Before announcing this Linux-based implementation, Amutable had released two open source tools. tine, a Buck2-based build system that composes OS images; available under MPL-2.0, and Quarry, a TUF-based tool that distributes those images to machines; available under Apache-2.0. What Amutable Foundations brings to the table is a service where Amutable runs the images, update repositories, control plane and vulnerability fixes for organizations and individuals who operate their own systems. No license has been stated for those company-run parts, and the preview announcement does not say how much of Foundations beyond tine and Quarry will be open source. Software and configuration both ship as signed images, and a machine boots only what is signed. Because there is no package manager and no SSH or interactive shell, a change means giving every machine in your fleet a new signed image. Its images are reproducible, come with a software bill of materials (SBOM) and are built from pinned inputs of whichever distribution you pick, with tine being able to import packages from Fedora or CentOS Stream. Each machine sends a signed report that records what it booted, from the firmware and boot loader up to the kernel and OS, plus the configuration it runs... Amutable's control plane verifies reports from systemd-report, an experimental command in systemd 260 that the company helped build. It compares each one with the declared state and flags machines that deviate. A rollout only continues while machines can show they are in a good state... Amutable is signup formlooking for teams that manage Linux fleets and need to show what state each machine is in. It expects to start bringing the first 'design partners,' as it calls them, on board over the coming weeks.

Bitcoin (BTC) lost about 7% in October, breaking the Uptober pattern of seasonal gains. The slide came as the artificial intelligence (AI) trade cracked.

Oil futures climbed as Treasury yields slid, breaking an 80% correlation seen this year. Meanwhile, Bitcoin dropped about 4% late Thursday, hours before Friday’s Nasdaq selloff and instep with the historic hack of Binance.

Why Did Oil and Bonds Stop Moving Together?

Jeffrey Rosenberg, a senior portfolio manager at BlackRock, traces the break to AI stocks. The Magnificent Seven, the largest AI-linked US companies, retreated through October.

“In the market’s mind, the geopolitical risk is secondary to AI risk.”

Rosenberg said this on CNBC.

The Magnificent Seven have had a tough October. Source: CNBC

In his view, markets now read AI weakness as a warning for US growth. That pulled yields lower even as oil climbed.

Rosenberg calls third-quarter gross domestic product (GDP) strong and AI-related. Business investment rose 12.7%, the biggest gain outside the pandemic since 2011.

However, Fed Chair Kevin Warsh disagreed on Wednesday. He argued that capital spending is broadening beyond narrow pockets.

Warsh also named rising oil as a factor in the central bank’s inflation fight. Rate futures had pointed to a December hike by mid-October, as Fed rate hike bets built.

Google, meanwhile, posted its largest one-day drop on record last week, erasing about $500 billion in value.

Does Bitcoin Uptober Still Matter When AI Drives Markets?

Historically, Bitcoin rose in 10 of 13 Octobers through 2025, with a median gain near 15%, data shows.

October 2025 also ended lower, so Bitcoin’s 2026 price decline marks a second straight red October.

BTC now sits under $80,000. Source: CoinGecko

Microsoft, in contrast, returned to the public bond market after nearly a decade. Credit default swap spreads, which price default insurance, sit near 55 for Microsoft. Oracle’s sit closer to 300.

Rosenberg says the bond market can absorb an expected $4 trillion of AI debt over four years. The cost would be lower prices and wider spreads. If that supply arrives, the AI buildout would sit at the center of bond pricing, not just equity sentiment.
