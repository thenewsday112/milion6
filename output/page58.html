Roma boss sends warning to Manchester United after record-breaking start to season

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →
Gian Piero Gasperini is loving what he is seeing from his players (Picture: Shutterstock)

Manchester United face a Roma team on Tuesday night that has had a blistering, record-breaking start to the Serie A season in terms of goals.

Gian Piero Gasperini’s side won 5-2 at Udinese on Saturday to continue their fine form which sees them unbeaten in 10 games at the start of the Serie A campaign.

In those 10 fixtures they have scored 28 goals, which is more than they had ever previously managed by this stage of the season.

Former Aston Villa forward Donyell Malen has been the star of the show, scoring eight league goals so far this season and 11 in 12 appearances across all competitions.

However, it has been a team effort, with no other player contributing more than three Serie A goals so far and the contributions of so many have pleased Gasperini.

He is happy to talk up how confident his team is ahead of a trip to Old Trafford on Tuesday, where confidence is not so easy to find.

‘Sometimes the numbers do tell you the story,’ he said after the dominant win at Udinese. ‘Besides scoring lots of goals, we’re also scoring with many different players.

Donyell Malen has been in stunning form so far this season for Roma (Picture: Shutterstock)

‘That shows how much confidence there is in the team: lots of players believe they can make a decisive contribution, be it with an assist or a goal. We’re scoring more goals with more players and that’s a great sign.’

Portuguese teenager Rodrigo Mora and 23-year-old defender Daniele Ghilardi were both on the scoresheet against Udinese on Saturday.

Gasperini has a number of exciting young players in his squad, with the likes of Emanuele Lulli, 19, Niccolo Pisilli, 22, Matias Soule, 23, and Santiago Castro, 22, all also scoring in Serie A this season.

‘I’m lucky to have all my players – even the not-so-young ones – who really listen to me,’ said the manager. ‘Youngsters nearly always do that because they’re keen to establish themselves and improve.

‘I’m lucky because we have a great mix of players. We may have other shortcomings but they all give me everything.’

Michael Carrick is not exuding much confidence at Manchester United (Picture: Press Association)

The feelings could hardly be more contrasting at Old Trafford as Manchester United made it nine games without a victory thanks to a 2-0 loss at Chelsea on Saturday.

The loss in west London, which saw Lisandro Martinez shown a red card, leaves Michael Carrick’s men 17th in the Premier League table, having also picked up just three points from three Champions League outings.

Carrick is expected to still be in charge on Tuesday against Roma, but this winless run cannot continue much longer for him to keep hold of his job.

Germany’s Largest Bank Prepares Crypto Custody Launch, With Asia Expansion to Follow

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

In a matter of weeks, Deutsche Bank, Germany’s largest bank, will roll out its first digital asset custody service. Data shared earlier today revealed that the bank aims to tap growth in the digital economy by launching a crypto custody platform in the coming weeks.

Offer direct crypto custody service for its institutional clients

According to the report, the institution will soon introduce a digital asset custody service. The new service will allow institutional and corporate clients to securely hold and transfer Bitcoin and Ethereum through Deutsche Bank, following regulatory approval.

According to the report, the bank’s phased rollout will at first support custody of ​top ⁠crypto assets, including Bitcoin and Ether, before broadening the service to include offerings that enable customers to move money into and out of major stablecoins and other leading cryptocurrencies.

Deutsche ​Bank also plans to allow clients to access tokenized assets, including digital bonds, equities, and many others, as per the announcement

Impact of MiCA regulation on EU financial markets

The recent European Union’s move to bring crypto assets into the mainstream institutional framework through groundbreaking MiCA regulation has had profound impacts on the wider capital market. For example, the legislation has boosted market integrity. The entry of major financial institutions into the cryptocurrency market has been rising significantly; this is just one of the key impacts. This shows a major shift, as several major European institutions want to hold crypto assets mostly offered by specialized crypto players.

Now, leading German bank Deutsche Bank is building its own infrastructure. In the next few weeks, its corporate customers will be able to trade Bitcoin and Ethereum directly through its core banking platform, without relying on third-party professional crypto platforms. This will substantially lower the barrier for institutional clients to participate in crypto trading, further fueling interest in Germany’s local crypto market.

After the crypto launch in Germany, Deutsche Bank intends to expand the custody platform into Asia’s financial hubs next year, the report revealed. This clearly shows that traditional banks are no longer bystanders. They are beginning to manage crypto assets for institutions themselves.

“Hard To Be Bearish Here” — Analyst Who Nailed XRP’s 700% Surge Turns Giga Bullish After Spotting Rare 2022 FTX Bottom Signal

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

XRP could be approaching another major turning point, according to a veteran trader who previously anticipated its staggering 700% rally in 2024. DonAlt is once again leaning heavily bullish after identifying a rare market reaction that reminds him of the November 2022 FTX collapse.

Despite the latest crypto market shock surrounding the historic $6.3 billion Binance security incident, Bitcoin and other major assets have shown resilience rather than extending their losses. For the analyst, that unexpected strength is a compelling reason to believe the market could be setting up for another significant recovery.

DonAlt Turns Giga Bullish as Market Resilience Challenges the Bears

For trader DonAlt, it is becoming difficult to remain bearish because the market is showing unexpected resilience despite a major negative catalyst. The U.S. FBI confirmed that North Korea’s TraderTraitor hacking group was behind last week’s theft of over $6 billion from Binance, the world’s largest crypto exchange. Rather than triggering an extended sell-off, the reported Binance security incident has been followed by signs that buyers may be absorbing selling pressure.  

Bitcoin showed resilience amid renewed market turbulence, holding near $77,900, while XRP remained relatively steady at around $1.25.

According to DonAlt, the current market behavior echoes what unfolded after the FTX collapse in November 2022. Bitcoin eventually bottomed around $16,000 following the crisis before embarking on a historic recovery that later carried it to $100,000. The analyst appears to see a similar opportunity in the market’s ability to withstand fresh negative headlines without triggering a deeper sell-off.

“Last time the market didn’t properly sell off after a huge event like this was the FTX collapse. When I saw the lack of follow-through, then I got giga bullish. And we subsequently went from 16k to 100k,” the trader posited. “Hard to be bearish here after having been given the same setup yet again.”

Is the Crypto Market Repeating Its November 2022 Playbook? 

DonAlt also pointed out that a familiar market setup does not guarantee an identical outcome. Responding to an X user who questioned whether history would repeat itself, the trader stressed that nothing in the market is certain and that trading ultimately comes down to playing the odds.

The pundit explained that he liked the risk-reward opportunity in 2022 and sees a similarly attractive setup today. While the trade could still go against him, he believes taking such opportunities consistently offers a greater chance of making money over time.

The coming weeks could prove decisive in determining whether this bullish outlook holds up. If Bitcoin and other major cryptocurrencies hold up after the Binance incident is fully priced in, it could strengthen the argument that selling pressure has largely been absorbed. Attention may then turn toward tracking the stolen funds as investigators monitor their movement through crypto mixers. However, if prices eventually give way, the current resilience could prove to be only a temporary pause before another wave of selling.
