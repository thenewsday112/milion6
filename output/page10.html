Rare 'Alien' Fish With See-Through Head Spotted Off California Coast (Video)

📅 Nov 02, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Somewhere nearly 2,000 feet below the surface off Central California, scientists just came face-to-face with a creature that looks less like a fish than something from outer space.

Meet the barreleye fish (Macropinna microstoma), a deep-sea oddity with a transparent, jelly-like head and two bright green, tubular eyes that appear to float inside its skull. It’s unsettling, fascinating, and—thanks to the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute (MBARI)—now captured by the organization in 4K for the first time.

Check out the video below.

The footage was recorded roughly 55 miles offshore, at a depth of about 1,970 feet, during an expedition aboard MBARI’s research vessel David Packard. Scientists were using the remotely operated vehicle Doc Ricketts to study gelatinous sea life when research specialist Shannon Johnson spotted the fish as the robot was heading back toward the surface.

The team stopped immediately. Crew members crowded into the control room for a look at the bizarre visitor.

The barreleye fish.

MBARI

“Seeing a barreleye fish is a rare treat,” said MBARI senior scientist Steven Haddock.

No kidding. Across nearly four decades, roughly 7,000 underwater robot dives, and more than 41,000 hours of footage, MBARI has recorded just 11 sightings of this species. The last came in 2021.

As freaky as its appearance is, that see-through dome serves a purpose. In the ocean’s dim “twilight zone,” the barreleye points its eyes upward to detect prey silhouetted above, then rotates them forward to track food. The gelatinous shield also protects those delicate eyes as the fish snatches crustaceans from the stinging tentacles of siphonophores.

Related: Woman Tries to Pet Great White Shark. It Doesn’t Go Well. (Video)

Researchers watched the fish for about 20 minutes, experimenting with lighting and even recording 3D imagery. They left it in the ocean.

Haddock described the animal as such:

“It is such an amazing creature, which looks like it was created by an imaginative child. If you compare historical drawings of trawled specimens, the transparent dome surrounding their eyes has been stripped away. I didn’t even think they were illustrating the same species. So to see it alive with the dome of its ‘cockpit’ intact is a real privilege.”

The footage, released October 28, offers a striking reminder that even California’s famously explored coastline hides animals most people will never see alive.

And it gives us a peek into another world, right here on earth.

Eminem Is Bringing Wu-Tang Clan to the Lions' Thanksgiving Halftime Show

📅 Nov 02, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Thanksgiving in Detroit comes with a few guarantees. Of course, there’s turkey, macaroni and cheese, questionable family conversations, you wishing you could sit at the children’s table and Lions football.

This year, Eminem is adding something nobody had on the menu—the Wu-Tang Clan.

Wu-Tang! Wu-Tang! Wu-Tang!

The Detroit Lions announced Sunday that the legendary New York hip-hop group from Staten Island will headline halftime of their Thanksgiving Day matchup against the Chicago Bears on November 26. Eminem and longtime manager Paul Rosenberg are executive-producing the show, continuing their partnership with the team.

Eminem overseeing Detroit’s Thanksgiving entertainment makes perfect sense. As far as Detroit royalty, he’s the main course, and the game is the dressing. You can be sure there will be plenty of other Detroit staple fixings.

But inviting Wu-Tang Clan is like inviting that new-to-town coworker to Thanksgiving dinner, telling them to bring something to drink, but instead they show up with sweet potato pie—when your family has served pumpkin for generations.

Then everybody takes a bite and realizes the new guy—who just told your mother-in-law she could call him “36 Chambers”—might have brought the best dessert on the table. Suddenly, Grandma’s pumpkin pie has some competition.

“We often like to do things that are both exciting and unexpected,” Rosenberg said in the team’s announcement. “A Wu-Tang Clan Thanksgiving performance in Detroit certainly fits that description!”

That’s putting it mildly.

Related: Nipsey Hussle Fans Just Got Something They’ve Waited 7 Years For

Last Thanksgiving, Detroit native Jack White headlined halftime, with Eminem making a surprise appearance. It was a distinctly Motor City affair. This year’s selection reaches across state lines to the East Coast, where RZA, Method Man, Ghostface Killah and company have spent more than three decades reminding listeners to protect their necks.

Not typically advice one expects between second helpings and a post-turkey nap, but here we are.

Wu-Tang arrives with plenty to celebrate. The group is part of the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame’s Class of 2026, with its induction ceremony scheduled for November 14, just 12 days before Thanksgiving.

Related: Jay-Z Closed His 30 Tour With Dr. Dre, Snoop Dogg—and a Warning About AI

Rosenberg said he and Eminem have been fans of the group since the early 1990s, so the booking isn’t entirely out of left field. It’s just a different flavor for a Detroit tradition.

And come halftime, that sweet potato pie might be everybody’s new favorite dessert. No disrespect to pumpkin.

The Lions’ 87th Thanksgiving Day Classic kicks off at 1 p.m. ET on CBS.
