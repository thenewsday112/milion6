Cardinale: “Milan were at the top of the world, and we’re going to bring them back there”

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

By: Oliver Fisher

AC Milan owner Gerry Cardinale spoke to CBS Sports before the derby win over Inter, pledging to bring the club back to the summit of football.

On Halloween night, the Rossoneri ensured that Inter’s derby nightmare continued as Milan won their fourth consecutive victory in the fixture, extending the unbeaten run against the Nerazzurri to eight meetings in the process.

All of the damage was done in the first half. Christian Pulisic struck in the fourth minute with a header, Adrien Rabiot scored from a corner six minutes later, Alessandro Bastoni halved the deficit and then Diego Moreira converted a lovely free-kick to reopen the two-goal advantage.

In the end, 3-1 is how it would stay, securing a memorable win. Cardinale spoke with the CBS panel in the build-up to kick-off at San Siro last night and he shed a bit more light on his plans for the Rossoneri moving forward. His words are via MilanNews.

What makes this Milan derby special?

“Well, first of all, this stadium… As an American, I can say that a stadium like this is something you never see anywhere else. Tonight, it’s going to light up. There’s something different about this derby tonight than in the past.

“There’s an electricity here that I’ve never felt before. You’ll see nearly 80,000 people literally shaking this structure. It’s going to be truly incredible.”

Photos by Carlo Pellegatti and Marco Luzzani/Getty Images

Is the new stadium project your ‘baby’?

“It is. You know, when we bought the club four years ago, I started working on this right away. And part of what we’re trying to do here is bring the best of America to Italian soccer, and specifically to AC Milan.

“It’s extraordinary: most people didn’t think we could accomplish all this, or even get to this point. Not only did we announce the stadium this week, but we also announced the largest naming rights deal in history. I think it’s a wonderful thing for Italian soccer.

“The message to everyone is that we’re coming back. Italian soccer truly deserves to be at the top again, and we’ll do everything we can through AC Milan to make that happen.”

How important is Pulisic to the project?

“There’s a connection between us. We’re two guys from Pennsylvania trying to make their way and understand Italy. He’s ‘Captain America’, but he’s also my franchise player. I couldn’t be more thrilled to have him with us.

“It’s a true partnership with him. He’s a leader, and his presence on the court makes all the difference in the world. You’ve seen it this year: when he steps on the court, he completely changes the game.”

Why is AC Milan and winning with Milan so important to you?

“I remember George Steinbrenner telling me about twenty years ago, ‘Don’t get too emotionally involved in all this; don’t go into the locker room, don’t do those things’. But in this case, I got emotionally involved.

“For me, it became a personal issue. And I don’t know what it is: maybe it’s just that… look around, listen. This means so much to the people of Milan, it has a huge impact on them, and I feel a huge responsibility.

“AC Milan were at the top of the world, and we’re going to bring them back there. This will probably be one of the greatest things I’ll do in my career.”

Photos: AC Milan

What does this atmosphere mean to you?

“It’s something multigenerational. This stadium is an icon. Look around, look at these people: this means everything to them. All of this carries with it an enormous amount of responsibility, it almost makes your wrists tremble.

“The best thing I can do is do my job well to help this club win, because victory spreads to the whole community. Listen… can you hear? That’s the Curva. You don’t see anything like it in America, it’s truly special.”

What’s your favourite detail of the new stadium?

“What we tried to do with the new stadium was to live up to the name and tradition of this facility. This is San Siro, but it is also ‘La Scala del Calcio’, the opera house of football.

“When you look at the design of the new stadium, those majestic, slender lines on the exterior recall the grand drapes and curtains of La Scala. It is our tribute to the history of this city.”

How did you manage to unblock the stadium situation?

“It’s been an incredible collaboration, a true partnership with our president Paolo Scaroni and the mayor. It’s extraordinary because everyone told me it’s impossible to build a stadium here in Italy. The last major stadium built in Italy was in 2011 in Turin, with about 40,000 seats.

“The extraordinary thing about our project is that it will be a 75,000-seat stadium, and we expect to have it completed before the 2032 European Championships. It will be a great thing for all of Italy.”

Bugatti CEO Chased His Stolen Chiron Across Borders in a Porsche 911

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Leaving your keys inside an unlocked vehicle is always a terrible idea, but doing so with a multi-million-dollar hypercar is a uniquely expensive gamble. Bugatti Rimac CEO Mate Rimac recently learned this lesson the hard way after a rather brazen car thief managed to steal his personal Bugatti Chiron directly from a company facility in Croatia. The incident unfolded casually, with security footage showing the suspect smoking a cigarette before wandering into the building, spotting the keys sitting inside the seven-figure machine, and simply driving off.

According to Rimac, he had developed a habit of leaving the keys inside his vehicles, perhaps to avoid the hassle of getting locked out. He likely assumed no one would be foolish enough to steal something so recognizable. Fortunately, an eagle-eyed employee quickly spotted the 1,479-horsepower hypercar speeding away from the factory and immediately alerted the rest of the team through a company group chat, confirming that an unauthorized joyride was indeed in progress.

View this post on Instagram

The Cross-Border Pursuit

We caught the drama as it happened on his Instagram page. Rather than simply sitting around and waiting for the authorities to handle the situation, the Bugatti CEO took matters into his own hands. Rimac immediately jumped behind the wheel of a nearby Porsche 911 that fortunately had a full tank of gas and began chasing down the suspect. While the thief already had roughly an hour head start and was actively fleeing toward Slovenia, Rimac had a massive technological advantage: the Chiron’s advanced live telemetry system.

This built-in factory software allowed Rimac and his engineering team to monitor the hypercar’s location, vehicle speed, and exact fuel level in real time. They continuously relayed these live updates to the police via their group chat, effectively turning the stolen Bugatti into an incredibly expensive tracking device. We wonder whether the stolen vehicle was Rimac’s notoriously high-mileage Chiron, though the executive was just relieved the situation remained under digital surveillance as the chase crossed international borders.

View this post on Instagram

Man Took his Shot

The cross-country pursuit finally came to an end after the thief crossed into Austria. The suspect had even stopped to put roughly half a tank of gas into the thirsty quad-turbo, 8.0-liter W16 engine, and naturally, he drove off without paying the fuel bill. Thanks to the constant barrage of live telemetry updates provided by Rimac’s team, Austrian police were eventually able to seamlessly intercept the stolen hypercar.

Fortunately, the Chiron only sustained very minor front bumper damage, avoiding the catastrophic wreckage that easily could have occurred. While I’m sure he must be glad it wasn’t his dream car, a V10-swapped BMW E30, the multi-million-dollar scare prompted an immediate policy change. The CEO has officially vowed to never leave keys inside his vehicles again, putting an end to his trusting factory habits.
