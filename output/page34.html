14-year-old hands himself into police over sickening Bradley Lowery taunt

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

A 14-year-old boy has handed himself into the police after taunting Sunderland fans with a picture of Bradley Lowery.

A video of the incident, taken during Coventry City’s 3-1 win over Sunderland on Saturday, was posted to social media and very quickly went viral.

An image of the late Bradley Lowery was used by a Coventry City fan to taunt Sunderland fans during the Sky Blues’ 3-1 victory on SaturdayGetty

The clip showed the teenage boy taunting the fans in the away end of the Coventry Building Society Arena by pointing to an image of Lowery that he had gotten up on his phone.

Lowery was a young Sunderland supporter who sadly passed away from stage 4 neuroblastoma – a rare and aggressive type of cancer – in 2017 at the age of six.

Prior to his passing, he and his family had forged a close, personal relationship with then-Black Cats striker Jermain Defoe, and also became the club’s mascot.

After the Sky Blues learned of this footage that was circulating, they very quickly released a statement which read: “Coventry City FC is aware of footage circulating on social media in connection with an incident during our Premier League fixture against Sunderland.

“The club does not tolerate offensive behaviour and will liaise with the relevant authorities to determine the appropriate course of action.”

Police confirm teenage boy hands himself in

Since then, the West Midlands Police has confirmed that the young fan has turned himself into the police.

“We are aware of a video circulating on social media showing an incident at Coventry City’s Premier League match with Sunderland yesterday afternoon (31 Oct),” their statement read.

“A thorough investigation has been launched, and a 14-year-old boy has since handed himself in and will be spoken with in due course.

“We understand the upset and outrage this video has caused in the community and to the victim’s family and our officers are keeping them up to date with the progress of our investigation and working closely alongside both football clubs.”

In the wake of his passing, Lowery’s mother Gemma, helped kickstart The Bradley Lowery Foundation, which raises funds to support families who are fundraising for treatment or equipment that is not covered by the NHS.

The Bradley Lowery Foundation was started in August 2017Getty Lowery and his family established a close relationship with then-Sunderland striker Jermain DefoeGetty

This was something the Lowery family had to do in an effort to raise money for Bradley to receive treatment that was only available in the USA – they would raise £1.3 million in their campaign.

The foundation also supports research into neuroblastoma and other childhood cancers.

Lowery’s mother pens emotional statement

Gemma also saw the video that circulated during the game, and took to social media through the foundation’s page, to issue a statement.

“My beautiful boy deserves to be remembered with love, not hate,” she wrote. “Yesterday, a Coventry fan used a photograph of Bradley to taunt Sunderland supporters. As Bradley’s mam, this is heart-breaking, especially as it’s one of my favourite photographs of him.

“Just look at that smile. I remember that day like it was yesterday. He was so happy, having so much fun. Those moments were precious because they were few and far between.

“So much of Bradley’s short life was spent battling cancer and enduring treatments no child should ever face. To see such a beautiful memory used in such a cruel way really does make my heart hurt.

Bradley’s mother Gemma penned an emotional statement after being made aware of the footage circulating on social mediaGetty

“We are a football-loving family who follow Sunderland home and away. I’m all for football banter, but surely mocking a child who died from cancer is not banter?”

But Gemma also recognised that the fan was just a child himself, and urged people not to retaliate with hate.

“I hope the club takes appropriate action and the young lad’s family help him understand how hurtful his actions were,” she continued. “I don’t want hatred directed towards him, so please please don’t, just for him to learn from this.

“Behind that photograph is a little boy who was loved beyond measure and a family who miss him every day. Thank you to everyone who has defended Bradley and shown us so much love. It means more than you know.”

After receiving an out-pouring of support, she issued a further statement doubling down on her stance for fans not to direct hate towards the teenager.

“I have spoken to West Midlands Police, who have confirmed that the young lad involved is currently in custody,” she wrote. “I have also been informed that he is only 14 years old, same age as Bradley would be now if he was still with us.

“With that in mind, I really want to urge everyone not to take matters into their own hands or direct any hate, threats or harm towards this young boy or his family. As hurtful and upsetting as his actions have been, he is still a child himself.

“The police and Coventry City Football Club will both be speaking with him, and I sincerely hope this is enough to make him understand the seriousness of his actions and the devastating impact that words and actions like these can have on families and friends.

“I don’t want hate to be met with more hate. That is not what Bradley stood for, and it is certainly not what I want in his name. I just hope this young lad learns a very important lesson from all of this and thinks twice before ever doing something so cruel again.”

Juan Atkins, Techno Pioneer and Cybotron Co-Founder, Dies at 64

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Juan Atkins, widely regarded as one of the founding fathers of techno music and a co-founder of the influential electronic group Cybotron, has died. He was 64.

Atkins’ agency, Greensmith Artists, announced his death Sunday (Nov. 1) on Instagram.

Atkins was found unresponsive in his hotel room in Mexico City on Saturday (Oct. 31), ahead of a scheduled performance at YuYu Cine Club, according to Resident Advisor. He was transported to a hospital, where he was pronounced dead shortly after arriving.

“His family is currently dealing with extremely difficult circumstances, and we kindly ask that their privacy be respected during this time,” Greensmith Artists founder Dragan Greensmith tells Billboard.

No cause of death has been reported.

Born in Detroit in 1962 and raised in nearby Belleville, Michigan, Atkins met fellow students Derrick May and Kevin Saunderson, with whom he would become part of the Belleville Three. Widely credited as architects of Detroit techno, the trio helped establish the Motor City as a global center for electronic music in the 1980s.

In 1980, Atkins joined forces with fellow producer Rik Davis to form the electronic music group Cybotron. The duo released their influential debut album, Enter, in 1983, featuring the seminal track “Clear,” before parting ways in 1985.

“Clear,” a meticulously crafted yet wildly inventive techno track influenced by Afrika Bambaataa and Kraftwerk, ranked No. 77 on Billboard‘s list of the 100 Best Dance Songs of All Time.

Following Cybotron’s breakup, Atkins launched his own label, Metroplex, in 1985 and began releasing music as Model 500. His first single under the name, “No UFO’s,” helped bring Detroit’s developing techno sound to the U.K., laying the groundwork for the 1988 compilation Techno! The New Dance Sound of Detroit, which featured Atkins’ track “Techno Music.”

In the decades that followed, Atkins continued releasing music under aliases such as Infiniti and Audio Tech, as well as his own name. He also teamed up with German producer Moritz von Oswald as Borderland and reunited with May and Saunderson as the Belleville Three, performing together around the world throughout the 2010s.

“Heartbreaking news today. My brother Juan Atkins has passed away,” Saunderson wrote on his Instagram Story Sunday. “Juan was the originator of Techno, a true visionary who opened the door for so many of us, myself included. I’ll never forget how he showed me how to finish my first record, Triangle of Love. That meant a lot to me then, and it still does today.”

He continued, “We shared a journey that changed music forever, and I’ll always be grateful for his friendship, guidance and everything he gave to our scene.”

Ole Gunnar Solskjaer has returned to coaching after missing out on a Manchester United comeback earlier this year.

The 53-year-old is working with the first-team and junior side at fifth-tier Norwegian outfit FCK.

Solskjaer has been coaching a fifth-tier club in Norway after missing out on a United returnAFP

It comes after Solskjaer was snubbed for the caretaker role at Old Trafford back in January.

The former Red Devils boss held talks over a return to the club following Ruben Amorim’s sacking.

However, Michael Carrick was ultimately appointed before signing a two-year permanent deal in May.

Solskjaer has since started working with local outfit FCK in Kristiansund.

The club are fresh from finishing ninth in the 11-team Norwegian Fourth Division.

Solskjaer eyes managerial return

A return to full-time management remains on the cards for Solskjaer, though.

Speaking to TV2 Sport, he admitted when asked: “Yes, I think I’ll probably take a job.”

As for whether he would consider taking the United job again in the future, Solskjaer added: “If Michael does well for ten years and decides to quit, and they ask me if I can take them for two months, of course I wouldn’t say no.

“I’ve been at that club for 18-19 years, so it’s part of the family. But it’s not something I’m thinking about.”

Solskjaer at Man United

Solskjaer spent three years in charge of the Red Devils between December 2018 and November 2021.

Solskjaer spent three years in charge of United between 2018 and 2021Getty Images - Getty

He also guided United to the Europa League final in 2021 before losing on penalties to Villarreal.

His stint as manager followed 11 years at Old Trafford as a player.
