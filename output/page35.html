FC 27 Pumpkin Seeds SBC tasks and solutions

The Pumpkin Seeds SBC has only one task to complete, so it’s not very difficult. However, the solo task has specific requirements you’ll need to meet to complete the challenge.

  • Plant the pumpkin seeds to earn a Large Gold Players Pack.
  • Same league: Max. four players
  • Same club: Min. three players
  • Clubs: Max. four
  • Quality: Min. seven Gold players
  • Team rating: Min. 70
  • Chemistry: Min. 22
  • Players: eight

Ideally, you will use cards that are already in your Ultimate Team squad. When you supply fodder from your own club, you do not need to spend any coins on the market. This saves your coins and increases the valuation of whatever rewards you get.

Alejandro Balde attracts Manchester United interest

Alejandro Balde of Barcelona (Photo by Alex Caparros/Getty Images)

The left-back has struggled to establish himself in Barcelona’s starting lineup this season, managing just 101 minutes in La Liga and 16 minutes in the Champions League.

Balde had initially been determined to fight for his place at the Catalan club, but his limited involvement has reportedly changed his stance. His relationship with Flick has deteriorated considerably, with the defender now considering a departure when the January window opens.

Alexis Mac Allister branded a ‘joke’ by Liverpool legend for Arsenal antics

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →
Alexis Mac Allister tried and failed to get Noni Madueke sent off (Picture: REUTERS)

Alexis Mac Allister’s attempts to get Noni Madueke sent off during Liverpool’s defeat to Arsenal have been branded a ‘joke’ and ’embarrassing’ by Reds legend Jamie Carragher.

Kai Havertz scored the only goal of the game at Anfield as the Gunners moved to the top of the Premier League table and Liverpool stayed fifth.

Andoni Iraola’s team had more possession, xG and shots than the visitors, but did not get a single one of their efforts on target.

As the home side pushed for an equaliser late on, tempers flared thanks to Mac Allister who sparked a dust-up.

Arsenal’s Martin Odegaard went down after a challenge with the Argentine, with Mac Allister then shouting something at the felled Norwegian before slapping him on the back of the head.

Jurrien Timber took umbrage with that and Madueke then got involved, squaring up to the Liverpool star and as their heads briefly met, Mac Allister went down holding his forehead.

Clearly it was an attempt to get the Gunners winger sent off, but neither the referee nor VAR were buying it and Madueke avoided any punishment.

Mac Allister initially clashed with Jurrien Timber (Picture: Getty Images)

Carragher made his feelings very clear on Mac Allister’s action, calling the World Cup winner ’embarrassing’ for his ‘joke’ of a response to the coming together.

‘I’m delighted he never got sent off there. We have got to be strong on calling people out and saying that this is embarrassing. I cant stand it,’ Carragher told Sky Sports.

‘I think everybody in the country watching football this head-to-head stuff. If someone puts their head back and butts, or course red card, but this stuff about head-to-head and if someone touches, the other one goes down and the payer gets sent off.

‘As a game, as football, people in the Premier League, supporters, call your own people out for it.

Mac Allister has been ridiculed for his reaction (Picture: Getty Images)

‘I’m a Liverpool fan, that’s a joke from Mac Allister but it’s got to stop. People are trying to gain an advantage because they know they can because red cards are getting given for it.

‘Whether it’s from Howard Webb, the Premier League, the managers get together. This has got to stop. Stop getting red cards for this crap!’

A social media post from the Premier League Match Centre confirmed why there was no action taken against Madueke.

It read: ‘The referee’s call of no violent conduct by Madueke on Mac Allister was checked and confirmed by VAR.

‘VAR: “All of the situations have been checked and cleared. There is some contact as Madueke and Mac Allister square-up. In my opinion, it’s not forceful, not violent, not brutal.”‘
